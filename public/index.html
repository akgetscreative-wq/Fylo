      Step-by-Step Hotspot Guide
      1
      Open Settings: Click the button below to open Windows Mobile Hotspot settings.
      2
      Turn Hotspot On: Toggle "Mobile Hotspot" to ON. 5 GHz band is recommended for fastest local transfer speeds.
      3
      Connect Phone: On your Android phone, connect Wi-Fi to your PC Hotspot name using the password shown in Windows Settings.
      4
      Scan & Transfer: Scan the Fylo QR code on your PC screen. Both devices will pair directly.
